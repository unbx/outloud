OUTLOUD / GETTING STARTED

How OutLoud works

Good moments deserve to be heard.

Every recording holds a moment worth sharing. OutLoud helps you find it, shape it, and make it heard.

OutLoud is a browser-based audio clipping and audiogram tool. Find a passage in a recording, trim it, add synchronized captions, and share it as video. When your audience speaks another language, dub the clip into a supported language while preserving the speaker’s voice and delivery.

From a recording to a moment worth sharing

  1. Input. Upload an audio or video file, record your microphone, or capture tab audio in a supported desktop browser.
  2. Clip. Preview and trim a passage. Analyze the recording for suggested moments and transcript search, or caption your chosen clip directly. Analysis is optional.
  3. Design. Add a creator name and image. Choose your format, caption layout, colors, and a digital or analog waveform visualizer.
  4. Share. Preview the result and download video, audio, or SRT / VTT captions.

Find the part people will remember.

A town hall cannot fit every timezone. A podcast conversation can hold one idea worth passing on. OutLoud helps community teams, audio producers, and creators turn those recordings into focused clips people can watch, read, and hear in their language.

Questions before you start

What is an audiogram?

An audiogram turns audio into a shareable video with a visualizer, captions, and optional creator imagery. OutLoud renders the composition in your browser.

Do I have to analyze a recording?

No. You can trim a passage or use the full recording and create captions directly. Analyze when you want suggested moments or transcript search.

Can I paste a podcast, YouTube, or Space URL?

Direct URL import is not available in this version. Upload a recording, use your microphone, or capture tab audio where supported.

Which languages and downloads are supported?

Use original-language captions or choose a supported dubbing language in the app. Download audio and SRT / VTT captions. Video format depends on the browser; check the Share tab for the available format.

What do I need to use OutLoud?

Try one recording up to 2 hours without a password: analyze it, add original-audio captions, design an audiogram, and download it. Free sessions have limited daily capacity and do not include dubbing. After making your first clip, request beta access with your email and X account. Existing tester passwords and your own API keys keep their current access. Recording and capture features depend on browser permissions and support.

Is all processing local?

No. The visual composition renders in your browser. Transcription, dubbing, and optional moment analysis use processing services. Open “How your data is handled” in the app for details before processing a recording.

OUTLOUD / YOUR DATA

How your data is handled

No accounts, no logins, no analytics or tracking cookies. Here's where things go:

  • Your API key is stored only in your browser and sent straight to ElevenLabs with each request. It never reaches NANA's servers. Disconnecting clears it.
  • Your recordings are sent to ElevenLabs when you request transcription or dubbing. Translations use Google Translate. NANA does not retain uploaded audio. Free-session transcript storage is described below.
  • Find moments sends recording sections to ElevenLabs for transcription, then the transcript and your brief through OutLoud to OpenAI for recommendations. For connected testers, OutLoud keeps the transcript in this tab only, until the recording is replaced or the page reloads. Free sessions also use temporary private server storage. An optional OpenAI key stays in page memory and passes through the server without being saved.
  • Your audiogram (captions, audio, and video) is rendered in your browser and downloaded by you. Nothing is uploaded to NANA.
  • Beta access requests send your email and X account to NANA via our email provider so Sean can review and respond to your request.
  • Free sessions use a temporary cookie and a hashed network identifier to limit usage. Audio is processed for captions; we temporarily store the transcript, analysis and session usage in private storage to support retries and prevent repeat charges. Records older than three days are removed during subsequent session creation. The free session lasts 24 hours.
  • Tester mode routes requests through a NANA proxy that adds the owner's key server-side. Your clip passes through on its way to ElevenLabs and isn't retained.

ElevenLabs, OpenAI and Google process data under their own policies. See OpenAI, ElevenLabs and Google.

Capture audio on your phone

Your phone can record whatever it is playing, including a Space replay in the X app. Record just the part you want, then upload the file here.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Add Screen Recording to Control Center once, in Settings, Control Center.
  2. Open the Space replay and get it ready to play.
  3. Swipe into Control Center and tap the record button. Leave the mic off, it records the app's own audio.
  4. Play the part you want, then tap the red status bar to stop. The video lands in Photos.
  5. Come back here, tap Clip, and pick that recording.

Android

  1. Swipe down twice and tap Screen record.
  2. Set audio to Device audio, then start.
  3. Play the part you want, then stop from the notification shade.
  4. Come back here, tap Clip, and pick the recording from your gallery.

OutLoud pulls the audio out of the video automatically, and Trim lets you tighten the selection after it loads.
